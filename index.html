The Roastery Concept

Precision engineering meets old-world craftsmanship.

At Velvet & Roast, coffee is an art of patience. We source exclusively from high-altitude farms above 1,600 meters, ensuring dense beans packed with rich organic sugars.

Every batch is roasted on our custom cast-iron drum roaster, profiling each origin to highlight its natural terroir—never over-roasting, never compromising.
